About Me

Who I am:

I am a 23 year old BSCS student in my graduating year at the University of the Cordilleras, Baguio City, Philippines. With a broad interest in various tech fields, I am continually exploring paths that resonate with me. My interests currently lie in full stack development and AI/ML.

Hobbies & Interests:

Outside of academics, I immerse myself in:

  • Playing Roblox and Minecraft
  • Cooking and baking
  • Watching anime
  • Traveling - I’ve been to Malaysia, Singapore, New Zealand, and Canada
  • Dancing, specifically in Latin ballroom, hip hop, and K-pop
  • Photography is another creative outlet I enjoy
  • I love pets, though I don’t currently have any

What I Value:

I aspire to find a balance between work and life, create meaningful and fun connections, and travel the world to experience new cultures.

Personality:

Friends describe me as hard-working and book smart. Despite being an introvert, I have a natural ability to lead and connect with others when needed. Additionally, according to the Myers-Briggs personality test, I am an INFJ-T (Advocate).
